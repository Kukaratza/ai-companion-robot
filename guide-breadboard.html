1Meet your breadboard

A breadboard is a board full of little holes. You push parts and wires into the holes. No glue, no soldering. But underneath, some holes are secretly joined by metal. Point at a hole (or tap it) to find out which ones!

👆 Point at any hole!
🧠 RememberHoles in the same column on the same side of the middle groove are joined. The long rails at the top and bottom are joined all the way along.

2The 5 golden rules

1️⃣
Columns are joined. Holes a b c d e in one column are one wire. Holes f g h i j are another.
2️⃣
The groove is a wall. The top half and bottom half are NOT joined. That's why chips straddle the groove.
3️⃣
Rails run far. The red and blue lines are joined along their whole length. We use the blue one as the ground highway.
4️⃣
One pin, one column. Two different pins must never share a column, or they get stuck together (a short circuit).
5️⃣
Unplug first! Pull out the USB cable before you move any wire.
🧩
To connect two things: put the ends in different parts of the board, and join them with a wire. Never put two pins side-by-side in the same column.

3The classic mistake 😱

Here are two wires. One carries the GPIO1 signal and the other carries GPIO2. Switch between the wrong way and the right way.

🔎 Do a "strip check"Before plugging in, look at every column that has a wire or pin. Ask: "Is there exactly one job in this column?" Side-by-side columns are fine, because each column is its own strip.

    5Wiring checklist

    Every wire in one place. Tick each one off as you plug it in. Wires are matched by name, like a matching game!

    ⚠️ Safety first!Always unplug the USB cable before changing any wire. Ask a grown-up to check your wires before you plug in. A wire in the wrong place can break a part.
    🙌 The brain has pins on BOTH sides!We use both, so the wires don't get tangled. Top half of the board: the amplifier and the button, plus the 5V power. Bottom half: the microphone and the face screen, plus the 3.3V power. The bottom parts are flipped over like a pancake 🥞, so their names read backwards.
